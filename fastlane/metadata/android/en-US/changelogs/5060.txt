Mermaid diagrams are drawn in answers. When an agent answers with a Mermaid diagram you see the diagram, not its source: flowcharts, sequence, state, ER and class diagrams, in light and dark. Try it: ask an agent to draw a flowchart in Mermaid, then tap it to zoom full screen. Wide diagrams scroll sideways; Copy source copies the text. Other kinds stay as code, with a line saying why.
